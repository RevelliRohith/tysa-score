Module or rule Required behaviour
D input Posture Perfection /6 and Stability/Grace /2. Optional detailed microfields: accuracy /4, mounting/dismounting /2, stability /1, grace/presentation /1. Time comes from the Time Judge.
Item score (D execution out of 8 + approved time out of 2) × base value. Compulsory BV = 1. Sum seven items for each D-JUDGE.
Panel calculation
Traditional: 5 D-JUDGES → remove one highest and one lowest full total, average remaining 3. 
Four → remove one highest and one lowest, average remaining 2. Three → average all 3, with 
no removal. Use the CD-confirmed performance panel count; wait for all assigned submissions. 
Subtract approved final-score penalties once. Reduced panels are the requested championship 
setting.
Time attempts Use COP Chapter 4 Annexure A/B timing tables. Under-75% hold and second-attempt rules affect item validity and deductions; do not implement only a simple linear formula.
Traditional T penalty box
No transition timer, transition countdown or automatic buzzer. Keep asana-hold timing. Add 
manual penalty amount, reason, item/attempt and deduction scope; Chief reviews. Apply once 
at the approved COP calculation step.
Tie-break Compare compulsory-asana marks using the same 5/4/3-judge averaging method as the performance panel. If still tied, support joint medal rather than an optional-asana tie-break.
Traditional examples before penalties: 5 judges with totals 50, 52, 54, 56, 58 → remove 50 and 58 → average = 54. Four 
judges with 50, 52, 54, 58 → remove 50 and 58 → average = 53. Three judges with 50, 52, 54 → keep all three → average 
= 52. Subtract an approved final-score penalty once: a penalty of 2 gives 52, 51 and 50 respectively.
Asana catalogue and validation
Import Annexures 1–3 as versioned data: code, name, image, category, level, BV and judging rules. Registration must 
allow the full catalogue, repeated asanas and any category combination without blocking save or submission. Show rule 
guidance for athlete information only. The Evaluator checks applicable age/round, compulsory/optional, category and 
repetition rules during the event. FBS and FBF share the Forward Bend major category.
Keep failed attainment, second attempts, support-related component reductions, short holds and manually recorded 
penalties as separate facts. The technical rule table determines their combination; unresolved placements are listed in 
the decision register.

Difficulty judging for music events
D-JUDGE workflow: open the assigned roll → receive pushed entry → mark every item → save/submit → correct and 
resubmit only after CD undo. Each music event has four D-JUDGES. Execution is out of 10 using the applicable COP micro-
marking criteria. Store original inputs, invalidity decisions, component reductions and the effective calculated item 
separately.
Event Required fields Each item calculation
Artistic Single Execution /10 and declared BV. Execution × BV.
Artistic Pair P1 execution /10, P1 BV; P2 execution /10, P2 BV; Formation /10. SP1 = EP1 × BV1; SP2 = EP2 × BV2; SP = (SP1 + SP2) / 2; pair item = (SP + Formation) / 2.
Rhythmic Pair P1 execution /10, P2 execution /10 and shared BV. ((EP1 + EP2) / 2) × shared BV. No separate Artistic Pair formation mark.
Module or rule Required behaviour
Full panel score Sum ten items for each D-JUDGE. Drop one highest and one lowest full total; average the remaining two.
Final formula Retained D average + average Artistry + average Timing − approved evaluator/other penalties. Deduct any overtime only once.
Invalid item
Accepted hold below 5 seconds, or a confirmed Evaluator Invalid Asana decision, makes that 
complete item zero for all four D-JUDGES. For pairs, zero the complete pair item. Preserve 
original marks; recalculate judge totals before discarding extremes.
Attempt/support rules
COP micro-marking includes second-attainment deductions and support-related component 
reductions. Provide applicable fields; do not automatically zero all components unless the rule 
requires it.
Technical recheck Chief can request review, but only CD can undo/unlock a submitted record. Original assigned judge then corrects and resubmits. Preserve history; Admin has no undo permission.
Artistic Pair example: P1 = 8 × 0.9 = 7.2; P2 = 7 × 0.8 = 5.6; Formation = 8. Item = ((7.2 + 5.6) / 2 + 8) / 2 = 7.2. If the 
accepted shared hold is below 5 seconds, the complete item becomes zero.
Rhythmic example: P1 = 8, P2 = 6, shared BV = 0.9. Item = ((8 + 6) / 2) × 0.9 = 6.3. Both players must perform the same 
asana, side/leg/hand and direction. Artistic Pair instead requires different asanas at the same time.

Artistry workflow: watch routine → enter components/deductions → save/submit → correct only after CD undo. Two 
Artistry Judges enter component marks and their applicable deductions/penalties separately. Average their net scores. 
Appeals distinguish Artistry penalties from subjective Artistry marks. Do not deduct an Artistry penalty a second time in 
the evaluator total.
Timing component Calculation
TW Whole Performance Below 150 seconds = 0; 150–160 = 3; 161–170 = 4; 171–180 = 5.
TA Holds Each valid hold of at least 5 accepted seconds = 0.5; ten items give maximum 5. Below 5 accepted seconds = 0 TA for that item and zero complete item for every D-JUDGE.
T1 and T2 Two Time Judges, each with two independent timers: TW and item hold. Average the two totals after applicable reconciliation.
Pair hold Measure the shared steady position as one unit, not the average of separate player hold durations.
Rounding Store raw readings; record whole seconds with 0.5 rounded up.
4 / 5 disagreement One judge records 4 and the other 5: accepted hold is 5. Both record 4: hold is invalid.
150-second margin COP allows a 3-second margin where one TW reading is below 150 and the other is 150 or more. Example 148 / 150 is treated as 150.
Overtime After 180 seconds, 0.5 marks per extra second. COP says disqualification after 10 seconds. Exact 190-second trigger and TW award above 180 need confirmation.
Time Judge workflow: receive entry → record TW and holds → submit → review flagged readings; correction requires CD 
undo. Example: both judges record 165 seconds and nine valid holds. TW 4 + TA 4.5 = Timing 8.5. After zeroing the invalid 
item, D totals 60/62/64/66 yield 63. Artistry 16 gives 87.5 before other penalties. Finishing below 150 removes only TW, 
not the entire final score.

Evaluator decisions and sequence records
Event Evaluator duties
Traditional 1 Evaluator Declared compulsory/optional order, performed items, category and sequence issues under the relevant round rules.
Artistic Single 1 Evaluator Declared ten-item order and BV; coverage of all ten subcategories; extra and undeclared asanas.
Artistic Pair 2 Evaluators E1 checks Player 1; E2 checks Player 2. Check five major categories, pair order and formation validity. Shared incidents are not charged twice.
Rhythmic Pair 1 Evaluator Checks both players; same asana/side/direction, declared order, category coverage and extra items.
Module or rule Required behaviour
Incident fields Performance/chest number; declared position/code/BV; observed position/code; P1/P2/both; incident type; affected item numbers; evidence note/time; reporting evaluator; Chief decision.
Sequence scale COP states first change 2 marks, second 4, third 6, more than three DQ. Confirm cumulative versus replacement totals and how moves/swaps count before automating multiple changes.
Music extra items First extra asana = 5 marks; second extra = DQ.
Single/Rhythmic categories Missing one/two/three subcategories = 5/10/15; more than three = DQ under COP-standard profile.
Artistic Pair categories Two items from each major category; less/more than two = 5 marks each occurrence under COP wording; missing more than three major categories = DQ.
Invalidity versus penalty
Confirmed Invalid Asana makes the affected complete D item zero for every D-JUDGE. Record 
item/player, reason and review. Numeric Penalty Marks are a separate action; a penalty alone 
must not automatically zero the item. Preserve original marks.
Formation checks Artistic Pair same-asana formation, syllabus/BAC/pyramid rules and final-position support must follow COP. Refer component reductions to D-JUDGES rather than replacing their inputs.
Sequence example: declared A–B–C–D, observed A–C–B–D. Record rows 2 and 3 as a linked swap with actual codes. 
Technical policy determines the incident count. If approved as one incident, 87.5 − 2 = 85.5. Do not infer one penalty per 
mismatched database row.
Evaluator workflow: compare the unrestricted declaration with the performance → check applicable COP rules, repeats, 
categories and sequence → record incident and affected item/player → submit for review → apply confirmed action. 
Registration acceptance does not waive a COP penalty or make an asana valid.

Chief review Jury appeals and final results
1. Each required judge submits a complete record. Empty fields remain Missing, not zero; intentional zero is a recorded 
value.
2. Chief checks missing seats, invalid items, time disagreements and evaluator incidents. Chief may request review; only 
CD can undo the submitted record before the original judge corrects it.
3. Chief approves provisional score; store first public display timestamp and produce the signed/approved score sheet.
4. Track appeal intake and disposition. Jury views completed scores by chest number. Authorised staff record the written 
decision and evidence without giving Jury score-edit permission.
5. Director approves the final result after appeal requirements are satisfied; publish the final signed/approved result and 
close the division.
Module or rule Required behaviour
Jury search
Chest alone returns all matching entries/events/rounds in the selected championship. Optional 
event narrows results. Either partner’s chest/linked ID retrieves the pair. Ask for selection if 
matches are ambiguous.
Jury detail D item records/totals; Artistry components; T1/T2 raw readings and marks; evaluator incidents; penalties; ranks; correction history. View only.
Appeal intake COP: prescribed written form, one performance/chest number per form, within 10 minutes of score display. Stage intake timestamp is recorded; attach form and receipt.
Appeal scope Timing, evaluator decisions and the penalty part of Artistry. D marks and Artistry marks themselves are not ordinary appeal targets. Chief-requested review is tracked separately.
Fee Uploaded COP specifies Rs. 1,000 or US $10 and says the fee can change. Publish the approved state championship fee in settings; do not hardcode it as a timeless price.
Director closure No unresolved required record or appeal may be silently bypassed. Block or require an explicit recorded technical decision. Closed results require a controlled correction/reissue process.
Jury view-only is your software access rule; it does not remove Jury’s COP decision authority. Store the signed decision 
through an authorised review-record user, then implement an approved correction through the responsible scoring role.
Progression: QF → SF is top 20 or 20%, whichever is fewer; SF → Final is top 10 or 50%, whichever is fewer. Confirm 
rounding, small fields and cutoff ties. Tie-break: Traditional compulsory score; music events average Artistry; if still equal, 
joint medal is supported.
